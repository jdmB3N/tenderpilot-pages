Privacy note

Plain English, short. Last updated November 2026.

What we store

  • Your company name, the email address you give us, what your business does, the countries you work in and the contract size you choose.
  • Which tenders we have already emailed you, so you never get the same one twice.

Why

Only to email you public tenders that match your business, because you asked us to by signing up. We don't sell or share your details.

Who processes it

Our website and sign-up list run on Cloudflare. Emails are sent through Brevo. Tender matching uses Google's Gemini AI, which sees your business description and public tender texts, never your email address.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any email in one click, or on our unsubscribe page. To have your details deleted completely, reply to any of our emails and ask; we'll do it within 30 days.

Tender data

Tender information comes from TED, the EU's official public procurement database. Always read the official notice before you apply.
